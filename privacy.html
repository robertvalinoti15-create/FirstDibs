Firstdibs ("we", "us") helps students discover new job and internship listings and get them by email. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. By using Firstdibs you agree to this policy.

Who can use Firstdibs

Firstdibs is intended for students aged 13 and older. If you are under 18, please use it only with a parent or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Account info — your name and email address when you sign up.
  • Your preferences — the fields, level, location, and alert settings you choose.
  • Application tracking — the jobs you save or mark as applied, and the statuses you set.
  • Resume text — only if you use the resume reviewer. It is sent for AI analysis and returned to you; we do not store your resume on our servers.
  • Basic usage data — standard technical logs from our hosting provider (e.g. IP address, browser) used to keep the service running and secure.

How we use it

  • To create your account and show you a personalized job feed.
  • To email you new job listings that match your alert (you can turn alerts off anytime).
  • To provide the resume review feature you request.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers we share data with

We use trusted third parties only to run Firstdibs:

  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Supabase — accounts and database storage.
  • Resend — sending alert emails.
  • Anthropic — AI processing of resume text you submit for review.

Job listings shown in Firstdibs come from companies' own public career pages. Firstdibs is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, those companies, LinkedIn, or Indeed.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off email alerts anytime in the app.
  • Request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account and data, by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL].
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (e.g. under GDPR or CCPA).

Data storage & retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. Some information is stored in your browser (for example, jobs you save on a device) and stays on that device until you clear it. You can delete your account at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy. We'll post the new version here and update the date above.
